Everything you need
to switch fast.

Smart grouping, visual thumbnails, and keyboard navigation.

Window Grouping

Windows are grouped by app into columns. Navigate across groups with arrow keys, pick the exact window you need.

Visual Thumbnails

See live previews of every window. Instantly recognize what you're switching to — no guessing from app icons alone.

Keyboard First

Hold to browse and release to switch, or quick-tap for sticky mode. Arrow keys, Enter, Escape — everything just works.

Instant

Shows up the moment you press your shortcut. No loading spinner, no delay. Thumbnails refresh in real-time.

Customizable

Appearance styles, shortcut keys, exceptions per app, multiple shortcuts — make it yours.

Open Source

Free forever. Read the code, contribute features, or fork it. Built in Swift, no telemetry, no accounts.

Two ways
to navigate.

Whether you hold or tap, grouping keeps your windows organized.

Hold Mode

Hold your shortcut, arrow through grouped windows, release to switch. Classic feel, instant action.

Sticky Mode

Quick-tap your shortcut. The panel stays open. Browse freely with arrow keys, Enter to select, Escape to dismiss.
